# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: [Insert Date]**

At Expense Planner, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide a better experience for all our users.

*   **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via Supabase.
*   **Financial Data:** We collect the data you manually enter into the application, including income, expenses, savings, budgets, and transaction amounts/currencies. **Note: We do not connect to your bank accounts directly; we only process the data you input.**
*   **Technical Data:** We may collect information such as your IP address, device type, and browser type through our infrastructure and third-party services (like Vercel and Supabase) to ensure security and service stability.
*   **Usage Data:** Information on how you interact with the application to help us improve its functionality.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
*   Provide, maintain, and improve the Expense Planner service.
*   Authenticate your identity and secure your account.
*   Calculate totals, budgets, and financial statistics based on your entries.
*   Monitor and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity.

### 3. Data Storage and Security
*   **Storage:** Your data is stored securely using Supabase.
*   **Security Measures:** We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and financial information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

### 4. Protection of Children's Data
In compliance with international standards and the requirements of the North Macedonian Agency:
*   We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent.
*   If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such information from our servers.
*   We encourage parents and guardians to monitor their children's online activities and use this service with supervision.

### 5. Third-Party Services
We use third-party service providers to power our application:
*   **Supabase:** For database management and authentication.
*   **Vercel:** For hosting and deployment.
These providers have their own privacy policies regarding how they handle data.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion
*   You may request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the application or by contacting us.
*   We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services.

### 7. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
*   Access the personal data we hold about you.
*   Request correction of inaccurate data.
*   Request deletion of your data.
*   Object to or restrict the processing of your data.
